## 💡 What is Crypto Factor?

Crypto Factor is a decentralised infrastructure platform for Web3. The dApp offers the following features on **DeFiMetaChain**:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **CFR Staking** | Stake CFR tokens and earn rewards |
| **cDFI Staking** | Deposit DFI, receive cDFI and earn Masternode rewards |
| **Swap** | Exchange tokens directly within the dApp |
| **Bridging** | Transfer CFR between different blockchains |
| **Liquidity Mining** | Liquidity mining with different pairs |

> 💡 cDFI Community Staking allows DeFiChain users to receive a cAsset receipt token "cDFI" and stake it to earn native DeFiChain Masternode rewards.

## ✅ Requirements

Before using the dApp, make sure you have:

- [ ] **MetaMask** installed (browser extension)
- [ ] **DeFiMetaChain network** added to MetaMask
- [ ] **DFI or CFR tokens** in your wallet
- [ ] Some **DFI for transaction fees** (gas)

> ⚠️ Without DFI for gas, no transactions can be made – even if you hold CFR or cDFI!

## 🔌 Step 1 – Open the dApp & Connect Wallet

### 1.1 – Open the dApp

Open your browser and go to the official Crypto Factor dApp:

```
https://dapp.crypto-factor.io/staking
```

> ⚠️ **Always type the URL manually!** Phishing sites imitate dApps with similar addresses.

### 1.2 – Connect Your Wallet

Click **"Connect Wallet"** on the homepage.

A popup will appear with wallet options. Select **"MetaMask"**.

### 1.3 – Confirm the Connection in MetaMask

MetaMask will open automatically and ask you to approve the connection:

1. Check the displayed **website address** – it must be `dapp.crypto-factor.io`
2. Select the **account** you want to connect
3. Click **"Connect"**

> ✅ Your wallet address will now appear in the top right of the dApp – you are connected!


---


## 📡 Step 2 – Select the Correct Network

The Crypto Factor dApp runs on **DeFiMetaChain**.

If MetaMask shows the wrong network, the dApp will prompt you to switch. Click **"Switch Network"** and confirm in MetaMask.

If the network is not detected automatically, switch manually:

```
MetaMask → Network Dropdown → DeFiMetaChain (mydefichain)
```

**DeFiMetaChain network details:**

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Network Name | `DeFiMetaChain` |
| RPC URL | `https://dmc.mydefichain.com/mainnet` |
| Chain ID | `1130` |
| Symbol | `DFI` |

> ✅ Once the correct network is active, the dApp will show your DFI and CFR balance.

## 🪙 Step 3 – Use CFR Staking

### 3.1 – Open the Staking Page

Click **"Staking"** in the navigation menu.

You will see your current CFR balance, the current APR and your staked tokens.

### 3.2 – Stake CFR

1. Enter the amount of CFR you want to stake
2. Click **"Stake"**
3. MetaMask will open with a transaction request
4. Check the **gas fees** (in DFI)
5. Click **"Confirm"**

> ⏳ The transaction will be confirmed on the blockchain within a few seconds.

### 3.3 – Claim Rewards

Earned rewards are shown under **"Rewards"** or **"Claim"**.

Click **"Claim"** → confirm in MetaMask → rewards are transferred to your wallet.


---


## 💎 Step 4 – Use cDFI Community Staking

cDFI Staking lets you earn **Masternode rewards** without running your own Masternode.

### 4.1 – Deposit DFI & Receive cDFI

1. Navigate to the **cDFI Staking** section
2. Enter the amount of DFI you want to deposit
3. You will receive **cDFI at a 1:1 ratio** (minus mint fee)
4. Click **"Mint"** or **"Deposit"**
5. Confirm the transaction in MetaMask

> 💡 DFI is swapped for cDFI (1:1), the staked token is cDFI, and rewards are paid out in DFI.

### 4.2 – Stake cDFI

1. Enter the amount of cDFI
2. Click **"Stake"**
3. Confirm in MetaMask

### 4.3 – Receive Rewards

Rewards come from Masternode earnings and are distributed automatically. Click **"Claim"** to collect them.

### 4.4 – Redeem cDFI (Withdraw)

1. Click **"Withdraw"** or **"Unstake"**
2. Enter the amount of cDFI
3. Confirm in MetaMask
4. DFI will be returned after the waiting period

> ⚠️ **Note waiting period:** Redeeming cDFI may take time, as Masternodes must first be resigned once the threshold is reached.

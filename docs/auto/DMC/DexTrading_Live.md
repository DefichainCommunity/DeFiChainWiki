## 💡 What is Dex Trading Live?

**Dex Trading Live (DTL)** is a community-driven DeFi project providing 100% self-custody solutions for interacting with DeFi protocols. The project is based on *DeFiMetaChain*.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **DTL Staking** | Stake DTL tokens in vaults and earn rewards |
| **Liquidity Mining** | Provide liquidity to DTL pools and earn yield |
| **The Wallet** | Self-custody wallet for multi-transactions in one block |
| **Dex Trading Masters** | Community trading competition |

**DTL Token Contract Address:**
```
0xa29aa48B4A67C965C708c01E9FcCaE39691bfC91
```

- [ ] **MetaMask** installed (browser extension)

## 🔌 Step 1 – Open the dApp & Connect Wallet

### 1.1 – Open the dApp

Open your browser and type the URL **manually**:

```
https://dapp.dex-trading.live/staking
```

> ⚠️ **Always type the URL manually!** Phishing sites imitate dApps with similar addresses.

### 1.2 – Connect Your Wallet

Click **"Connect Wallet"** and select **"MetaMask"**.

### 1.3 – Confirm the Connection in MetaMask

MetaMask will open automatically:

1. Check the displayed website address – must be `dapp.dex-trading.live`
2. Select your account
3. Click **"Connect"**

> ✅ Your wallet address will appear at the top of the dApp – connection active!


---


## 📡 Step 2 – Select the Correct Network

The dApp runs on **DeFiMetaChain**. If MetaMask shows the wrong network:

Click **"Switch Network"** and confirm in MetaMask

> ✅ Once the correct network is active, the dApp will show your DTL balance.

## 🪙 Step 3 – DTL Token Staking

DTL Staking uses a **vault system** with lock periods – the longer you stake, the higher the rewards.

### 3.1 – Open the Staking Page

Navigate to the staking page:

```
https://dapp.dex-trading.live/staking
```

You will see:
- Your **DTL balance**
- Current **APR / reward rate**
- Your **active vaults** with lock periods

### 3.2 – Approve the Token (first time only)

The **first time**, you need to allow MetaMask to use your DTL tokens:

1. Click **"Approve"**
2. MetaMask opens with an approval request
3. Check the token (`DTL`) and amount
4. Click **"Confirm"**

> ℹ️ Only needed once per token – costs a small gas fee in DFI.

### 3.3 – Create a Vault & Stake

1. Choose your **lock period**
2. Enter the **DTL amount**
3. Click **"Stake"**
4. Confirm in MetaMask

> ✅ Your vault is active! Rewards accumulate automatically.

> 💡 **Tip – Multiple Vaults:** Want different lock periods? Simply use a different wallet address for each vault!

## 💧 Step 4 – Liquidity Mining

With Liquidity Mining you provide token pairs as liquidity and earn trading fees and DTL rewards in return.

### 4.1 – Open the Liquidity Mining Page

```
https://dapp.dex-trading.live/liquidity-mining
```

### 4.2 – Choose a Pool

Select one of the available pools

### 4.3 – Add Liquidity

1. Enter the amount of the **first token**
2. The second token amount is calculated automatically
3. Click **"Add Liquidity"**
4. Approve both tokens in MetaMask (once each, first time only)
5. Confirm the transaction in MetaMask

> ✅ You receive **LP tokens** as proof of your liquidity – these represent your share of the pool.

### 4.4 – Remove Liquidity

1. Click **"Remove Liquidity"**
2. Enter the LP token amount
3. Confirm in MetaMask

> ⚠️ Be aware of **Impermanent Loss** when providing liquidity – strong price movements can reduce the value of your position.


---


## 🎁 Step 5 – Claim Rewards

### Claim Rewards

1. Open your active vault on the staking page
2. Check your **accumulated rewards**
3. Click **"Claim"**
4. Confirm in MetaMask

> ✅ Rewards are transferred directly to your wallet!

## 💡 What is the TAX Token?

**TAX** is a cross-chain memecoin in the DeFiChain ecosystem, running on **DeFiMetaChain.The staking dApp is built on the Crypto Factor infrastructure.

| Property | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Token** | TAX |
| **Type** | Cross-Chain Memecoin |
| **Networks** | DeFiMetaChain (DMC) |
| **Function** | Staking for rewards |
| **dApp** | [dapp.tax-token.com](https://dapp.tax-token.com/staking) |

> 💡 TAX is available as a cross-chain memecoin on multiple blockchains. Make sure you have selected the correct network in MetaMask before using the dApp.

## ✅ Requirements

- [ ] **MetaMask** installed (browser extension)
- [ ] **DeFiMetaChain** network added to MetaMask
- [ ] **TAX tokens** in your wallet
- [ ] Some **DFI** (on DMC) for gas fees

> ⚠️ Without gas tokens, no transactions can be made!

## 🔌 Step 1 – Open the dApp & Connect Wallet

### 1.1 – Open the dApp

Open your browser and type the URL manually:

```
https://dapp.tax-token.com/staking
```

> ⚠️ **Always type the URL manually!** Phishing sites imitate dApps with similar addresses.

### 1.2 – Connect Your Wallet

Click **"Connect Wallet"** on the homepage and select **"MetaMask"**.

### 1.3 – Confirm the Connection in MetaMask

MetaMask will open automatically:

1. Check the displayed website address:
```
dapp.tax-token.com
```
2. Select your account
3. Click **"Connect"**

> ✅ Your wallet address will appear in the top right of the dApp – connection active!


---


## 📡 Step 2 – Select the Correct Network

```
MetaMask → Network Dropdown → DeFiMetaChain (mydefichain)
```

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Network Name | `DeFiMetaChain` |
| RPC URL | `https://dmc.mydefichain.com/mainnet` |
| Chain ID | `1130` |
| Symbol | `DFI` |

## 🪙 Step 3 – Stake TAX Tokens

### 3.1 – Open the Staking Page

You are already on the staking page at `/staking`. You will see:
- Your TAX balance
- Your currently staked amount
- The current APR / reward rate

### 3.2 – Approve the Token (first time only)

On **first use**, you need to allow MetaMask to use your TAX tokens:

1. Click **"Approve"**
2. MetaMask opens with an approval request
3. Check: Token = `TAX`, Website = `dapp.tax-token.com`
4. Click **"Confirm"**

> ℹ️ The approval transaction is a one-time step and costs a small gas fee. It only needs to be done once per token.

### 3.3 – Enter the Amount

Enter the **TAX amount** you want to stake, or click **"Max"** to stake everything.

### 3.4 – Confirm the Stake

1. Click **"Stake"**
2. MetaMask opens with the transaction request
3. Check: amount and gas fees
4. Click **"Confirm"**

> ✅ Your TAX tokens are now staked – you will see the updated amount in the dApp!


---


## 🎁 Step 4 – Claim Rewards

Staked TAX tokens automatically generate rewards. These are displayed under **"Rewards"** or **"Claimable"**.

1. Click **"Claim"** or **"Collect Rewards"**
2. MetaMask opens with the transaction request
3. Click **"Confirm"**

> ✅ Rewards are transferred directly to your wallet!


---


## 🔓 Step 5 – Unstake (Withdraw Tokens)

To withdraw your TAX tokens:

1. Click **"Unstake"** or **"Withdraw"**
2. Enter the amount you want to withdraw (or click **"Max"**)
3. MetaMask opens with the transaction request
4. Click **"Confirm"**

> ⚠️ Check if a **lock-up period** applies before unstaking. Some staking protocols have a waiting period before tokens can be withdrawn.
